NEON CUT is a poster studio that runs entirely on your own machine: drop in a few photos, pick one of five
panel layouts and one of four grades, letter a caption, and export a 1080 × 1080, 1080 × 1350 or 1920 × 1080
PNG. Nothing is uploaded and no request is made — the page is static files, the fonts are shipped with it, and
the photos never leave the tab.

A cover is drawn in one pass. Every panel is cut from its photo with its own focus point and zoom, graded
(sunset, neon duotone, dusk, noir), then textured with the same halftone screen and grain, its exposure pulled
towards the sheet's mean so the collage reads as one printed page, and pasted with a white keyline and a hard
offset shadow. The caption is fitted by ink, not by guesswork: each split of the title into one or two lines is
tried at every size, and Anton takes the largest that fits its box, over a hairline rule, a warm kicker and a
closing studio mark.

## Run it

```sh
node serve.ts
# open http://localhost:8080

mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```
